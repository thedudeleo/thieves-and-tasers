Description

Thieves and Tasers is a fast-paced 2v3 asymmetrical PvP heist game. Two thieves use parkour and stealth to steal treasure and reach extraction, while three guards lock the place down with security cameras, laser traps, and tasers. Matches swing between tense stealth and chaotic action — quick rounds built to play with friends.

Key features

  • 2v3 asymmetrical gameplay tuned toward a 50/50 win rate
  • Parkour-driven thief movement
  • Guard toolkit: cameras, laser traps, tasers, handcuffs
  • Online & LAN, with Family Sharing
  • Easy to pick up, with depth that rewards mastery
