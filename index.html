Two sides. One heist.

Every match splits five players into two crews with opposite goals. Pick a side — or play both.

2 Players

The Thieves

Climb walls, vault rooftops, and stay in the shadows. Grab the treasure and reach the extraction point before the clock — or the guards — catch up.

  • Parkour through the level
  • Avoid cameras & laser traps
  • Grab loot, race to extraction
  • Free a cuffed teammate for the clutch
VS
3 Players

The Guards

Lock the place down. Watch the cameras, set your traps, and zap thieves on sight. Cuff them before they slip away with the goods.

  • Monitor security cameras
  • Deploy laser traps
  • Stun with your taser
  • Handcuff downed thieves
