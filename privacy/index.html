Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This site (thievesandtasers.com) is the official page for the game Thieves and Tasers, operated by Arctic Palm Games ("we", "us"). We've kept this page intentionally simple because the site itself is too.

The short version

This is a static informational website. We don't ask for your name, email, or any other personal details. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter, and no advertising or analytics tracking that we run ourselves.

Information we collect

We do not directly collect personal information from you. There is nothing on this site for you to fill in or submit.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may automatically record standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in its server logs to deliver the site and protect against abuse. This is handled under GitHub's privacy statement.

Third-party content

A few features on this site are provided by third parties. When your browser loads them, those companies may receive your IP address and set their own cookies, governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — the site's typefaces are served from Google's servers, which receive your IP address to deliver the fonts. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Steam wishlist widget — the "Wishlist on Steam" widget is embedded from Valve's Steam store and may set cookies. (Valve Privacy Policy)
  • Social links — links to our Discord, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube take you to those external platforms, each with its own privacy policy.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies of our own. The third-party content described above may set cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information through this site, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you have a privacy question, contact us and we'll help where we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or game evolves. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@thievesandtasers.com.
